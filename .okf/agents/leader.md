# Leader Playbook

- Keep requested work aligned with `PRODUCT_VISION.md` and its explicit scope.
- Define the user outcome and the smallest useful, verifiable slice.
- Identify decisions that change product behavior or public contracts; record
  accepted durable decisions in `.okf/decisions/`.
- Protect backend authority for booking, access control, and room lifecycle rules.
- Do not silently promote an optional demo feature into a product requirement.
